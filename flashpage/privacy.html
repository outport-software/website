Flashpage

Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Flashpage is made by Outport Software in Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. It turns your study pages into flashcards on your device, and we designed it so your pages and cards stay with you.

Your photos, text and cards

Photos you take or choose, the text Flashpage reads from them, and the cards it writes are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. Flashpage has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not track you.

Downloads

The first time you set Flashpage up, it downloads the artificial intelligence models it needs (about 2.6 GB) from Hugging Face, a model hosting service. These downloads contain no photos or personal information. As with any download, Hugging Face can see your device's internet address.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple's App Store. The app checks your unlock directly with them on your phone. We do not run a purchase server, and no other company receives your purchase record.

Permissions

Flashpage asks for the camera so you can photograph a page. When you choose a photo instead, your phone's own photo picker shares only the photo you pick.

Children

Flashpage collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
