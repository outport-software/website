Flashpage

Snap a page. Get flashcards.

Photograph your notes, a textbook page or a handout. Flashpage reads the page and writes question and answer cards for you to check, then helps you study them a few minutes a day, bringing each card back just before you would forget it. Everything happens on your phone, even offline. Pay once; no subscription.

Need help?

Email support@outportsoftware.ca. Tell us what you were trying to do and, if you can, which phone you use. We read every message.

Common questions

Why does it download so much the first time?
Flashpage reads pages and writes cards with artificial intelligence models that run on your phone, so nothing you photograph is uploaded. They download once (about 2.6 GB) and are kept. Wi-Fi is best.

Which phones can write cards?
Writing cards needs a phone with at least 8 GB of memory: an iPhone 15 Pro, any iPhone 16 or newer, or a recent Android flagship. On other phones you can still type your own cards and study them.

A card is wrong.
Every card is shown for checking before you save it. Edit or remove it there, or later from the deck.

The page was read badly.
Lay the page flat in good light and fill the photo with it. Printed text works best. You can also fix the text before making cards, or paste text instead of taking a photo.

What do Again, Hard, Good and Easy do?
They tell Flashpage how well you knew the answer. Each shows when the card will come back. Good is the usual answer.

I bought the unlock on another phone.
Open Settings in Flashpage and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Google or Apple account.
