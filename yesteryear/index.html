Yesteryear

Bring old photos back to life

Yesteryear repairs scratches and tears, adds natural color to black and white photos, and sharpens photos so they are ready to print. Everything happens on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.

Need help?

Email support@outportsoftware.ca. Tell us what you were trying to do and, if you can, which phone you use. We read every message.

Common questions

Why does a feature download something the first time?
Each feature uses an artificial intelligence model that runs on your phone. It downloads once, the first time you use that feature, and is kept for next time. Wi-Fi is best, because some are large.

Is my original photo changed?
No. Yesteryear saves the restored photo as a new picture and leaves your original untouched.

I bought the unlock on another phone.
Open Settings in Yesteryear and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Google or Apple account.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store, so refunds go through them. We are happy to help if you email us.
