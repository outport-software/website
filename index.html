How we build

Four plain rules.

  • On your device

    The AI models run on your phone, not on a server. After a one-time download of the models, the apps keep working offline.

  • Private

    No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your photos, pages and saved games stay with you. This website has no cookies and no tracking either.

  • Pay once

    Try it free, then unlock it for good with one purchase. No subscriptions.

  • Made in Newfoundland

    An outport is a small fishing community on this coast. We try to build the same way: small, sturdy and useful, with large text, clear buttons and plain words.
