Tower Idle

Privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Tower Idle is made by Outport Software Inc. in Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. It is a single player game that runs on your device, and we designed it so that nothing about you or how you play leaves it.

Your progress

Your saved game and your settings are stored only on your device, in the game's own private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We keep no copy, so removing the game from your device removes your progress.

Information we collect

None. Tower Idle has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not track you.

Internet

Tower Idle does not connect to the internet. It works offline, and it downloads nothing after you install it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple's App Store. We do not run a purchase server, and we never see your payment details.

Permissions

Tower Idle does not ask for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. It can use your phone's vibration for touch feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

Tower Idle is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
