Tower Idle

Hold the coast against the storm.

A tower defense game about a lighthouse, a string of northern islands and a sky full of aurora. Build towers on the platforms along the shore, start a wave, and stop the storm before it reaches the beacon.

No ads. No account. Your progress is saved on your device, and the game works without an internet connection.

The game

A calm night watch, with a storm to hold back.

Build your defense

  • Five tower types: Pulse, Rail, Frost, Mortar and Arc. Each does one job well.
  • Buy damage ranks for each tower, then choose one of two specializations.
  • Set targeting to First, Last, Strong or Close.
  • Sell a tower for most of what you paid, and rebuild between waves.

Fight through the islands

  • Three islands to defend: Harbour, Headland and Beacon.
  • Five kinds of storm enemies, boss waves and thirty hand-made waves.
  • Saga mode carries on with new islands once the campaign is behind you.
  • Speed up combat to 2x or 4x, or pause to plan.

An idle game that respects your time

  • Once your farm is certified it earns salvage whether the game is open or closed.
  • Spend salvage in the Workshop and on research to make your next run stronger.
  • Start over when you want to, to earn lasting bonuses.
  • Waves can start automatically, so you can sit back and watch.

Calm to look at and listen to

  • A night-sea look with moving aurora, weather and a sweeping lighthouse beam.
  • All sound effects are made from scratch for the game.
  • Volume, haptics and effects quality settings, including a battery-saving mode.

Help

Common questions

When can I play it?

Tower Idle is still being built and tested, and it has no release date yet. This page will be updated when it does.

Does it need the internet?

No. The game does not connect to the internet at all.

Where is my progress kept?

On your device only. We keep no copy, so removing the game removes your progress.

Are there ads or an account?

No ads, no account, and nothing is collected. See the privacy policy.

Need help?

Email support@outportsoftware.ca. Tell us what you were trying to do and, if you can, which phone you use. We read every message.
