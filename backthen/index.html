Back Then

Keep the family stories

Back Then records the stories of a life, in the voice of the person telling them. The phone types each story up, you add old photos, and Back Then makes a book you can print and share. Everything happens on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

Need help?

Email support@outportsoftware.ca. Tell us what you were trying to do and, if you can, which phone you use. We read every message.

Common questions

Why does it download something the first time I type up a story?
Back Then types stories up with an artificial intelligence model that runs on your phone. It downloads once, the first time, and is kept for next time. Wi-Fi is best, because it is large.

The typed words are not quite right.
Open the story and tap the words to fix them. Names and places are the hardest for the phone to hear, so it is worth a quick read. Your changes are kept.

How do I print the book?
Tap Make the book. Back Then makes a PDF file. You can print it at home, save it, email it to family, or take it to a print shop.

What happens to my stories if I get a new phone?
Stories are kept in Back Then's folder on your phone, which your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) includes. Making the book is also a good way to keep a copy.

I bought the unlock on another phone.
Open Settings in Back Then and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Google or Apple account.
