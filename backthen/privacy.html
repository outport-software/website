Back Then

Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Back Then is made by Outport Software in Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. It keeps family stories on your device, and we designed it so they stay with you.

Your recordings, words and photos

Recordings you make in Back Then, the words it types up, and photos you add are stored only on your device and processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. They leave your device only if you choose to share them, for example when you share the book.

Information we collect

None. Back Then has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not track you.

Downloads

The first time you type up a story, Back Then downloads the artificial intelligence model it needs from Hugging Face, a model hosting service. These downloads contain no recordings or personal information. As with any download, Hugging Face can see your device's internet address.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple's App Store. To confirm and restore your unlock, our purchase service, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase record. It does not receive your name, email address, recordings or photos.

Permissions

Back Then asks for the microphone so it can record stories. When you add a photo, your phone's own photo picker shares only the photo you pick.

Children

Back Then is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
