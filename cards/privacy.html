Cards

Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Cards is made by Outport Software in Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. It turns your study pages into flashcards on your device, and we designed it so your pages and cards stay with you.

Your photos, text and cards

Photos you take or choose, the text Cards reads from them, and the cards it writes are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. Cards has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not track you.

Downloads

The first time you set Cards up, it downloads the artificial intelligence models it needs (about 2.6 GB) from Hugging Face, a model hosting service. These downloads contain no photos or personal information. As with any download, Hugging Face can see your device's internet address.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple's App Store. To confirm and restore your unlock, our purchase service, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase record. It does not receive your name, email address, photos or cards.

Permissions

Cards asks for the camera so you can photograph a page. When you choose a photo instead, your phone's own photo picker shares only the photo you pick.

Children

Cards collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
